# Preparing the Next Harvest

The rains have been generous this season. Our fields in the Kenyan highlands are alive with the deep green of sweet potato vines stretching across rich, red soil.

## The Process

This week, we focused on:

- **Soil preparation** — turning and aerating the beds to ensure optimal drainage
- **Slip planting** — carefully placing each vine cutting at the perfect depth
- **Organic mulching** — layering natural materials to retain moisture and suppress weeds

## Why It Matters

Every harvest begins with intention. We don't rush the process. The soil needs time to breathe, the slips need time to root, and the purple pigments need time to develop their full, rich color.

> "Patience is the most important ingredient in organic farming."

We're excited for what this season will bring. Follow our journey as we document every step from soil to table.
